First release of Traevy.

Track your commute with one tap and see how much of it you actually lose to traffic. Traevy records your route, then splits your time into moving versus stuck — and marks the slow stretches on the map, so you can see not just how long you sat in traffic but where. Weekly trends show whether your commute is genuinely getting worse.

Works fully offline. Signing in is optional and only used to back up your trips.
