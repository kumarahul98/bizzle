<objective>
Remove `android.permission.USE_EXACT_ALARM` from Traevy so the app becomes eligible
for Google Play's app content declaration, by switching the only two things that
required it — the weekly summary alarm and the per-weekday reminder alarms — from
exact to inexact scheduling.

Purpose: Google Play rejected the content declaration outright. `USE_EXACT_ALARM`
is restricted to apps whose *core functionality* is "calendar" or "alarm clock".
Traevy is a commute tracker, so it is not eligible and the permission must go from
all tracks. This is a hard Play requirement, not a design preference.

Output: Both `zonedSchedule` call sites on `inexactAllowWhileIdle`, the permission
and its now-false comment block gone from the manifest, and two regression guards
so neither can silently come back.
</objective>

**1. The enum variant exists and has exactly the semantics D-1 relies on.**
From `flutter_local_notifications-21.0.0/lib/src/platform_specifics/android/schedule_mode.dart`:

```dart
/// Used to specify that the notification should be scheduled to be shown at
/// the exact time specified and will execute whilst device is in
/// low-power idle mode.
exactAllowWhileIdle,

/// Used to specify that the notification should be scheduled to be shown at
/// at roughly specified time but may not execute whilst device is in
/// low-power idle mode.
inexact,

/// Used to specify that the notification should be scheduled to be shown at
/// at roughly specified time and will execute whilst device is in
/// low-power idle mode.
inexactAllowWhileIdle,
```

So `inexactAllowWhileIdle` **retains Doze wake-up** and gives up **only** exact
timing. This is precisely why D-1 chose it over plain `inexact` — plain `inexact`
does NOT execute during Doze and would silently break overnight and idle-device
reminders. Do not "simplify" it to `inexact`.

**2. The complete set of exact-alarm references in the repo is 5 lines, nothing more.**
